5) BusLoop
BusLoop (BusNow) is a real-time bus tracking and coordination system designed for closed environments like colleges, companies, or campuses.

It solves a very practical problem:

People don’t know where their bus is, and staff don’t have an efficient way to manage or communicate updates.

🚍 Core Idea (In Simple Terms)

There are two sides to the system:

1. User App (BusNow)

Used by students/employees/passengers.

See live bus location
Know ETA (Estimated Time of Arrival)
View assigned routes and stops
Get updates or alerts (delays, changes, etc.)

👉 Basically: “When is my bus coming?” — solved.

2. Staff App (Driver/Admin Side)

Used by drivers or transport managers.

Start/stop a trip
Share live GPS location
Update bus status
Manage routes or notify users

👉 Basically: “Let me broadcast where the bus is and manage trips easily.”

🔄 How They Work Together
The driver app sends live location data
That data is stored (backend like Supabase/Firebase)
The user app reads and displays it in real time

So:

Driver moves → Location updates → Users see bus moving live

That’s the core loop (hence BusLoop).

⚙️ What Problem You’re Solving

You can explain it like this:

No more waiting blindly for buses
No more calling drivers
Better time management for users
Easier control for transport staff
🚀 Why It’s Useful
Saves time daily
Reduces confusion and frustration
Scales well for campuses or companies
Can later expand into:
Notifications
Seat tracking
Route optimization
